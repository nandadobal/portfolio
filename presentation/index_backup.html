01
Bia Care
Co-founder · Health · 0→1
The Problem

Menopause care
in the UK was broken.

The market was fragmented. HRT had a reputational problem. Women were navigating a complex system with no continuity of care: GP appointments that lasted 10 minutes, or expensive private consultations with no follow-through.

There was no tech-forward, whole-person approach that combined clinical oversight with lifestyle intervention.

The Product

The first private
group consultation
clinic in the UK.

Built the full infrastructure for virtual group consultations: patient matching into cohorts, EMR integration, video consultation rooms, clinical note-taking, and end-to-end prescription workflows. CQC registered. Clinical governance from day one.

A team of four. A regulated clinic. Built and launched in months.

The Pivot

We killed a product
with an NPS of 86.

Our first product was a 12-week lifestyle programme. The pilot worked. The waiting list looked strong. But when the time came to pay, conversion was low.

What people said they wanted and what they were willing to pay for were different things. We killed it and refocused on the clinical core.

This failure made me significantly more commercial. Test willingness to pay early. Follow the money signal, not the satisfaction score.

Batelle
VP of Product · Consumer AI · Gen AI 0→1
The Problem

Premium sleep coaching.
100% human-powered.

Batelle paired parents with expert sleep guides — personalised programmes, 24/7 messaging, in-app video consultations. The model worked. NPS was high. Refund rates were low. Market size was capped.

When OpenAI released their API in late 2022, we had a narrow window. The product was proven. The question was whether AI could preserve what made it premium — and make it scale.

Design Insight 1

The content didn't change.
Making it feel personal did.

We launched a freemium app where the underlying content was the same across users. We made the personalisation visible — the app surfaced it explicitly rather than letting it work silently.

Free-to-paid conversion increased 13%. People don't respond to personalisation that works. They respond to personalisation they can see.

Design Insight 2

People didn't know
what to ask.
So we put the
invitation in the content.

Most AI products sit behind a chat tab. We embedded conversation prompts directly inside each lesson — contextual, in the moment, one tap away.

Not a support feature. A reading companion. Not escalation — engagement.

The Impact
84%
of messages handled entirely by AI at launch
Down from 100% human dependency
2022 Among the first D2C
Gen AI consumer products
$300–450 Validated price point
for the scaled product
The human-in-the-loop wasn't a compromise. It was a product decision. Keeping humans visible built trust, enabled escalation, and preserved the premium experience.
Why this matters for Neko?

A parent handing their child's sleep to an AI is the hardest trust problem in consumer AI. I solved it in 2022, before AI was mainstream, using customer obsession, built-in evals and humans-in-the-loop.

03
Cleo
Product Director, Chat & AI · Fintech · Platform
The Signal

Cleo's AI could only
talk about what it
had been programmed for.

The architecture was brittle. Siloed teams, fragmented tech, a conversational layer no single person fully understood. The product needed to become something fundamentally different: an assistant that works in the background, watches accounts, surfaces the right action at the right moment.

The existing architecture made that impossible. And the window to act was closing.

Before: Cleo 2.0
The Bet

Tools-based.
Multi-agent.
A year early.

Every Cleo product (savings, EWA, BNPL, credit) exposed as a tool the AI could call when the moment was right. An orchestrator decides which agent responds. This is the architecture the industry is now converging on.

I didn't ask for permission upfront. I built a shadow squad, ran a proof of concept, and brought the CEO on the journey, not to a decision. Then coordinated rollout across a pillar of 50, doing the PM work myself while setting direction for the larger organisation.

After: Cleo 3.0
What It Unlocked

The Financial Assistant:
an AI that works
for you in the background.

Not a chatbot. An engagement loop built around real moments in a user's financial life.

Trigger → Insight → Action → Reward. Payday hits. Cleo notices you've got £620 after bills. Proposes moving £200 to your France trip. You're now 70% there.

That loop only exists because of the tools-based foundation. Every new Cleo product plugs in as a tool. No rebuild required.

The Impact

The architectural decision has strategic compounding value: every new capability now slots in as a tool. The Financial Assistant is now the distribution channel for every product Cleo builds.
Why this matters for Neko?

Money and health provoke the same anxiety. Both need an intelligence layer that earns trust not by claiming it, but by surfacing the right action at the right moment. I built that for financial services and I'd like to build it for healthcare.
