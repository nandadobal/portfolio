02
Batelle
VP of Product · Consumer AI · Gen AI 0→1
The Opportunity

Premium sleep coaching.
100% human-powered.

Batelle's personalised programmes we human run, with 24/7 messaging. NPS was high and refund rates were low, but the market was limited.
Meanwhile, Open AI's APIs had just become available for product builders.

The Bet

Within 4 weeks, we built an eval loop
with a brand new technology.

Sleep guides were suggested generative messages. Within six months, our message approval rate went from 26% to >70%. The efficiency gain was good, but the knowledge base was the real asset.

First Breakthrough

Parents didn't know how to use AI,
so we taught them.

Using profile data, Elle reached out proactively. The ratio shifted from 2:1 to 4:1 in favour of the AI.

Second Breakthrough

Content and chat were disconnected.
So we put the conversation
inside the lesson.

Not a support feature. A reading companion. Engagement doubled in some cohorts.

The Impact

People came for the human.
They stayed for the AI.

26→70%+ Suggestion approval rate
users acting on AI prompts
80 → 16% Messages reaching a human,
at launch
$300–450 Validated price point
for the scaled product
The real asset was the feedback loop. Parents and AI growing the knowledge base. Coaches and AI growing the knowledge base. The more data, the better it gets. The more it fits the user, the harder it is to leave.
Why this matters for Neko?

A parent handing their child's sleep to an AI is a high-trust ask. Someone receiving a scan result that says "early signs of X" is higher stakes still. The design principles I used in 2022 — make the AI visible, keep the human accessible, build the escalation path before you need it — those apply directly to what you're building.

Cleo
Product Director, Chat & AI · Fintech · Platform
The Opportunity

Cleo had moments of brilliance.
Then forgot you existed.

Cleo went viral with her sassy Roast Mode, but every interaction was transactional, we didn't have an architecture that supported user progress and financial health.

The Bet

Tools-based.
Multi-agent.
A year early.

The architecture the industry has since converged on.

Before: Cleo 2.0
After: Cleo 3.0

Within a couple of weeks, we built a working prototype that was enough to get CEO buy-in. I ran a squad to get us far enough before moving the whole 50-person team across.

What It Unlocked

Chat is a surface.
Financial Assistant is the job.

The new infrastructure allowed us to move to an engagement loop that helps a user progress: Trigger → Insight → Action → Reward.

When Cleo notices you've got £620 after bills, she suggests moving £200 to your France trip so you don't have to worry.

The architecture unlocked an intelligence layer.
Engagement compounds.

Why this matters for Neko?

Money and health provoke the same anxiety. Both need an intelligence layer that earns trust not by claiming it, but by surfacing the right action at the right moment. I built that for financial services and I'd like to build it for healthcare.

General-purpose AI
"My LDL is 112. What does that mean?"
An LDL of 112 mg/dL is considered near-optimal to borderline high, depending on your cardiovascular risk factors. The American Heart Association recommends keeping LDL below 100 mg/dL for most adults.

Lifestyle changes that may help include reducing saturated fat, increasing soluble fiber, and regular aerobic exercise.
